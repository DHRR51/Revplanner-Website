What it is

Fit, scored against the ICP you define.

Most prospecting tools score a company on generic firmographics someone else decided matter. Prospect Match scores it against the ICP you set, your target industries, company sizes, revenue ranges, regions, and buyer titles, and shows you exactly which criteria matched so you can trust the call instead of taking it on faith.

Bring a prospect or a whole list and see which ones fit, and which criteria matched. The answer comes from the ICP you defined, not a stranger's model.

How it works

Three steps.

1

Bring a prospect or a list

Import a CSV of accounts you are considering, or point it at one company.

2

Score against your ICP

ProvenLoop checks each account's industry, size, revenue, region, and buyer title against the ICP you set.

3

See the why

Each match shows which criteria matched, traced to the imported row, so you know where to spend your time.
