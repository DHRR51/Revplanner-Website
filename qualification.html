Questions about Qualification

Which frameworks are supported?
SPICED, MEDDPICC, MEDDIC, BANT, SPIN and Challenger, chosen once in Company Context. BANT is used if you do not set one.
When does it score?
The Qualification Checker scores each call and the whole deal after every call, so the gaps surface on their own.
What is ICP fit?
A grade on each room against your Company Context: Strong, Possible, Weak, or Not enough data yet, the same grading Prospect Match uses.
What about things it has not heard?
What a call has not covered stays blank, never guessed. You see exactly what is still unproven.
