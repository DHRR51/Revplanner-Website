Do I need a CRM?

Not to start. ProvenLoop is the step before one.

Most founding AEs track deals in a spreadsheet, their inbox, or a CRM they set up once and never updated, because a CRM is data entry nobody has time for. ProvenLoop skips the data entry: your calls build each deal for you, so you have clean, organized deals from your very first conversation.

Have a CRM? ProvenLoop keeps it updated from your calls. Don't have one? You don't need it.

CRM alternative for founders

Questions a founding AE asks

I don't have time to keep a CRM up to date.
You don't have to keep it up. The Account Room updates itself after every call. The deal, the people, and both sides' promises are captured from what was said, so there is nothing to maintain by hand.
Is this just another CRM?
No. We are not a CRM. ProvenLoop keeps the deal from your calls, and if you have a CRM it gets the clean summary. The point is you don't need one to start.
What happens when we buy a CRM?
That is the plan. Connect it, preview what will be created, and click Sync. Your companies, contacts and call notes go in clean, matched by domain and email so there are no duplicates. No cleanup project, no import spreadsheet.
Why would my data be cleaner?
Because your first CRM data comes from what was actually said and who was actually on the call, not from someone typing on a Friday afternoon. Every field shows where it came from.
I already paste my calls into ChatGPT.
ChatGPT forgets the last call. ProvenLoop remembers every call on every deal, and who promised what, so you can ask about any deal in plain English and get the answer with the quote.
