Prompt for sales call notes

Today's copy-paste loop

It works, once, for the call in front of you. Then you do it all again on the next call, and nothing carries over.

1Copy the transcriptPull it from whatever recorded the call.
2Paste it in with your promptInto ChatGPT or Claude, with the prompt you keep in a note somewhere.
3Copy the pieces back outSummary into a doc, action items into a to-do list, the email into Gmail.
4Next call, start overNew chat, paste again, re-explain who everyone is and what happened last time.
Repeat for every call, on every deal, forever. Nothing is shared between those chats.
Why a chatbot alone falls short

Not because it is bad. Because it starts from zero.

✕

It forgets

Every chat starts fresh. It does not know your last call, your other deals, or what you promised a week ago unless you paste it all again.

✕

Not repeatable

The same question gets a different answer depending on the prompt, the day and what was pasted. You cannot build a process on that.

✕

Results scatter

Who owes what, by when, who decides, all end up spread across chats, docs and notes. Nothing tracks them, so things slip.

✕

No pattern across deals

It cannot see across deals, so it cannot tell you which ones fit your ICP, or what your best customers share.

✕

No proof

A chatbot can make up a quote or a date. You have no line to check it against.

✕

Training starts over

You can ask it to role-play or grade you, but it has never heard your buyer and keeps no record of how you did last time.
