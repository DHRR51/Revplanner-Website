What it is

The moments that matter, pulled out and ready to use.

A long call has a few moments that actually count: the objection the buyer raised, the commitment they made, the signal that the deal is moving. Clips find those moments and capture each one as the quoted text, its timestamp, and the call it came from, so you can use the exact words instead of paraphrasing from memory.

Clips are created automatically from every call, and you can make your own. They live in the Clips surface and drop straight into an Account Room's content or into a follow-up you build in Agent Chat.
