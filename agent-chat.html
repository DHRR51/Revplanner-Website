What it is

The one place you turn a call into the work.

Agent Chat is grounded in your Account Rooms, your company context, and your CRM. Ask it "what is stalling this deal," "how have I handled this objection," or "prep me for my next call," and it answers from what was actually said, with every claim traceable to the source.

Then it does the work. Tell it to build and it turns a call into a follow-up, a one-sheeter, a deck, or a role play, each as a draft you review and accept. Nothing is invented. When it does not have the evidence, it says so instead of making something up.

How it works

Three steps.

1

Point it at a deal

Start from an Account Room or a specific call so the answer is grounded in that context.

2

Ask, or tell it to build

A question gets a sourced answer. A request gets a drafted follow-up, deck, one-sheeter, or role play.

3

Review and send

Every draft shows its receipts. You accept what is right, edit what is not, and send.

Who uses it, and how

Three ways people run on it.

Solo AE

After-call admin, done

The follow-up, the commitments, and the deck for the next meeting are drafted before you close the laptop, grounded in what the buyer actually said.

BDR / SDR

Prep and outreach

Ask for a pre-call brief, or draft outreach that references the real conversation instead of a generic template.

Enablement

Turn a win into training

Convert a winning call into a role play that behaves like the real buyer, so a new rep practices against the deal, not a script.
